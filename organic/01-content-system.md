# Organic Content System

## What changes

The content is good. Three changes make it convert.

### 1. The 4:1 rule
Every fifth post makes a **direct offer**. Currently almost every post ends in
"watch the episode" or "link in bio." Educational content with no periodic ask trains
an audience to consume and never convert.

The offer is always low-commitment and honest:
- "Free 15-minute security review — we'll tell you what's exposed, even if you never hire us."
- "Free site walkthrough — we'll tell you what your cameras already do before you spend."

### 2. Tell the story natively
Move from 10–17s teasers to **45–60s native video** that completes the story on
platform. Link second, not first. Keep YouTube for the long build-alongs where depth
is the point. Watch time on-platform is what earns reach; pushing people to YouTube
in 10 seconds gives that away.

### 3. Rotate the caption shape
Currently near-every IG caption is hook → ✅ list → link in bio → sign-off → hashtags.
Rotate: **story** (no bullets) / **single question** / **list** (max every third).

## Fortnightly rhythm

Anchored on Tech Tuesday, which already works.

| Week | Post | Pillar | Format |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 Tue | Tech Tuesday episode | rotate | Native 45–60s + link |
| 1 Thu | Incident story or admission | Stay Secure | Story caption |
| 1 Sat | Behind the scenes / team / partner | Brand | Photo or short |
| 2 Tue | Data or teardown | See More | Native video |
| 2 Thu | **OFFER POST** | rotate | Direct CTA |
| 2 Sat | Single question | rotate | Text or short |

Six posts a fortnight. Sustainable solo, and one clear ask per cycle.

## Pillar balance

Roughly **40% Stay Secure / 30% See More / 20% Respond Faster / 10% brand.**
Surveillance is currently under-represented relative to its revenue potential and its
lower competition — deliberately over-index it for the next quarter.

## Hashtags — rebuild these

Stop using `#CyberSecurity` `#MSP` `#ManagedIT` as primaries. Far too large to rank in.

**Formula: 3 broad / 5 niche / 2 local.**

*Stay Secure:* #CyberSecurity #SmallBusinessAustralia #ITSupport · #EssentialEight #ACSC #PhishingAwareness #SMBSecurity #IncidentResponse · #SydneyBusiness #NSWBusiness

*See More:* #Surveillance #RetailSecurity #BusinessSecurity · #LossPrevention #AISurveillance #RetailTheft #CCTVAustralia #WarehouseSecurity · #SydneyRetail #AustralianRetail

## Fix the Buffer channel limit
5 channels connected on a 4-channel plan; YouTube is locked. Either upgrade or post
YouTube manually. Low priority — YouTube is a hosting location, not a distribution channel.

## Re-cut the CrowdStrike post
The 6 Aug partnership post was posted natively and framed as an internal thank-you.
CrowdStrike is a serious credential. Reframe for the buyer:

> "We're now a CrowdStrike partner. That means our clients get the same endpoint
> protection the ASX 200 runs — at small-business scale. Here's what that actually
> changes for you."

Partner credentials are trust assets. Make them about the customer, not the partnership.
