# Meta & Instagram Ad Copy — Launch

For the campaign 1 intro video (Peter to camera, service overview).
All fields validated against Meta's ad limits.

> **The caption is carrying the persuasion here.** The video is a service overview —
> it states what Alphalogix does, not why anyone should care. That's fine, but it
> means the primary text has to supply the hook, the problem and the offer. Don't
> shorten it to a line about services; that would leave the ad with no argument at all.

---

## Meta field limits

| Field | Limit | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Primary text | ~125 chars visible | Truncates to "See more" on mobile |
| Headline | 40 chars | |
| Description | 30 chars | Often hidden on some placements |
| CTA button | Preset list | Use **Book Now** |

**Everything below is inside these limits.** The first ~125 characters are the only
part most people read — each variant front-loads the hook there.

## Variant A — The honest angle ★ RECOMMENDED

Most on-brand, and the closest match to the positioning.

**Primary text**
```
Most IT providers will tell you you're covered.

Here's the honest version: almost every breach we're called into starts the same way. One click. One reused password. One old account nobody switched off.

Not sophisticated. Just preventable.

Managed IT, cyber security and surveillance — one Sydney partner, every Australian state.

Book a free 15-minute review. We'll tell you what's actually exposed, even if you never hire us.
```

- **Headline** (24/40): `Free 15-Minute IT Review`
- **Description** (28/30): `Sydney based, Australia wide`
- **CTA:** Book Now

## Variant B — The one-partner angle

Leads with the structural wedge. Best where multi-site buyers are in the audience.

**Primary text**
```
Your IT company doesn't do cameras. Your camera company can't touch your IT.

That gap is where the problems live — the network your cameras sit on, the storage, the remote access, the exposure.

Alphalogix does both. Managed IT, cyber security and AI surveillance from one Sydney-based partner.

Microsoft Partner. Built to ACSC Essential Eight.

Book a free 15-minute review.
```

- **Headline** (23/40): `One Partner. Not Three.`
- **Description** (24/30): `IT, cyber & surveillance`
- **CTA:** Book Now

## Variant C — The problem angle

Highest urgency. Opens on a question, which lifts comments and therefore reach.

**Primary text**
```
How long would your business survive without email tomorrow morning?

Not a scare tactic — a planning question. Most owners have never actually been asked it.

We're Alphalogix. Managed IT, cyber security and surveillance for Australian businesses since 2022.

Book a free 15-minute review and we'll give you a straight answer on where you stand.
```

- **Headline** (24/40): `What's Actually Exposed?`
- **Description** (21/30): `Free 15-minute review`
- **CTA:** Book Now

## How to run them

**Launch all three in one ad set, same video.** Same creative, three primary texts —
that isolates the copy as the only variable, so you learn which message works rather
than guessing.

Give it 5–7 days and at least 1,000 impressions per variant before judging. Kill the
weakest, keep the winner as the control, then challenge it with a new variant.

**Judge on cost per lead, not on clicks.** A variant can win on CTR and lose on leads
by attracting the wrong people.

## Before you hit publish

- [ ] **Booking destination decided** — a link, or Meta Instant Form? The copy says
      "book", so the click has to land somewhere that lets them book. A contact page
      is not a booking page.
- [ ] **Instagram bio link updated** to match the offer
- [ ] **Lead Gen ToS accepted** if using Instant Forms
- [ ] **Pixel firing** — otherwise this traffic is unretargetable and the spend is
      half wasted
- [ ] **Someone owns the 5-minute callback.** The copy promises a straight answer.
      A lead that waits until tomorrow undoes the whole message.

## One line to keep

**"even if you never hire us"** appears in A, and in both organic captions. It is the
single highest-value phrase in this copy — it's the entire brand in five words, and
it's what makes a sceptical owner book. Don't let anyone trim it for length.
