## How these are written

Every script is a **two-column edit sheet**: spoken line on the left, what's on
screen on the right. Hand the right column to whoever cuts the video.

## Assumption flagged

I haven't seen the footage. These are written for the most likely material —
Peter to camera, plus office/rack/site b-roll. Each script marks b-roll as
`[B-ROLL: …]` with a **fallback** where the shot may not exist. If a shot isn't
there, use the fallback; nothing here requires a reshoot.

**Send me a shot list or the raw files and I'll re-cut these to match exactly.**

## Rules that apply to all of them

1. **First 3 seconds carry the whole ad.** No logo, no "Hi, I'm…", no slow build.
   Open on the claim.
2. **Burned-in captions, always.** Most views are silent. Captions are not optional.
3. **9:16 vertical is the master.** Cut 1:1 and 16:9 from it. Keep faces and text
   inside the centre 80% so nothing is cropped by UI.
4. **Logo at the end only**, under 1.5 seconds. Front-loaded logos cost watch time.
5. **One idea per video.** If it needs "and also," it's a second video.
6. **No stock footage of hooded hackers or green code.** It reads as fake and
   destroys the exact trust the brand is built on. Real offices, real racks, real
   cameras, real people.
