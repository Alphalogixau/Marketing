# Script 01 — Cyber Hero (45s)
**Pillar:** Stay Secure · **Audience:** Cold, AU SMB decision-makers · **Placement:** Meta Feed / Reels

Working title: *"The Honest Version"*

| # | Spoken (Peter, to camera) | On screen |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | "I'm going to tell you something most IT companies won't." | **0–3s.** Peter, mid-shot, direct to lens. No music yet. Caption large: **"Most IT companies won't tell you this."** |
| 2 | "If someone's told you you're covered because you've got antivirus and a firewall — you're not covered. That's a starting point, not a strategy." | 3–11s. Hold on Peter. Music enters low. |
| 3 | "Because almost every breach we get called into isn't sophisticated. Someone clicked a link in an email. A password got reused. An old staff account was never switched off." | 11–21s. `[B-ROLL: over-shoulder of a laptop, an email opening]` → `[B-ROLL: hand on mouse]`. **Fallback:** stay on Peter, cut to three text cards — "One click." / "One reused password." / "One account nobody closed." |
| 4 | "That's it. That's how it happens. And it's all preventable." | 21–25s. Back to Peter. Beat of silence before "preventable." |
| 5 | "We're Alphalogix. We do this for businesses in every state in Australia — monitoring, email and endpoint protection, staff training, and a real person who picks up the phone." | 25–35s. `[B-ROLL: team at desks / server rack / phone being answered]`. **Fallback:** Peter + clean credential cards: Microsoft Partner · CrowdStrike Partner · ACSC Essential Eight |
| 6 | "Book a free fifteen-minute security review. We'll tell you what's actually exposed — even if you never hire us." | 35–43s. Peter, direct to lens. Caption: **"Free 15-minute security review"** |
| 7 | — | 43–45s. Logo + **See More. Respond Faster. Stay Secure.** + "alphalogix.com.au" |

### Why it's built this way

Line 1 buys the first three seconds by promising an admission, not a pitch.
Line 3 is the trust engine — it *lowers* the perceived threat (not sophisticated
hackers, just basic hygiene), which is both true and disarming, where competitors
inflate the threat to sell.
Line 6 is the whole brand in one sentence. **Do not cut "even if you never hire us."**
It is the single highest-value line in the script and the reason a sceptical
business owner books.
