# Script 05 — Founder Trust Piece (60s)
**Use:** Retargeting only (people who watched 50%+ of Script 01 or 03), plus LinkedIn
organic and the website. **Do not run this cold** — it's a closer, not an opener.

Working title: *"Why We Do It This Way"*

| # | Spoken (Peter, to camera, seated, conversational) | On screen |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | "I've been in IT about twenty years. In that time I've watched a lot of businesses get sold things they didn't need." | **0–7s.** Peter seated. Warmer, slower than the ads. Caption: **"20 years in IT."** |
| 2 | "Someone comes in, runs a scan, produces a red report, and there's a quote attached to it. The report's designed to sell the quote." | 7–16s. Hold. Let it be uncomfortable. |
| 3 | "We started Alphalogix in 2022 to do it the other way round. Tell people what's actually wrong. Tell them what they don't need. Then quote for the rest." | 16–26s. `[B-ROLL: team working]`. **Fallback:** stay on Peter. |
| 4 | "That's why we publish our incidents. Real client attacks, what happened, what we missed the first time, how we fixed it. Including the mistakes." | 26–36s. `[B-ROLL: screen-record scroll of the blog / Tech Tuesday thumbnails]` — this footage definitely exists. |
| 5 | "You can't fake that. Either you've done the work or you haven't." | 36–41s. Peter, still. |
| 6 | "So — managed IT, cyber security, surveillance, across every state in Australia. If you want a straight answer about where you actually stand, book fifteen minutes with us." | 41–55s. Three pillar cards: See More / Respond Faster / Stay Secure. |
| 7 | — | 55–60s. Logo + tagline + alphalogix.com.au |

### Note

Line 4 is the strongest line available to this business. The published incident
write-ups — including the hidden Outlook rule found *after* the first fix — are
verifiable proof. Most competitors would bury that. Publishing it is the whole
argument. Say it plainly and don't decorate it.
