### Why it's built this way

The possum line is deliberate. It's specific, Australian, and slightly funny — it
survives the scroll where "advanced AI analytics" doesn't. It also demonstrates the
actual product benefit (false alarm reduction) better than any spec sheet.

Line 5 is the honesty move: telling a prospect they may not need to buy anything.
It costs a small number of sales and wins a large number of walkthroughs. For this
brand it's not a risk — it's the entire proposition. **Keep it.**
