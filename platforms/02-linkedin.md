# LinkedIn — Organic Now, Paid Later

## Decision: no paid LinkedIn this quarter

At $500–1,500/mo total, LinkedIn paid is not viable. Australian B2B CPMs run high and
minimum viable spend is ~$50–80/day *per campaign* before delivery stabilises. Splitting
$33/day across two platforms means neither exits the learning phase.

**Revisit when monthly spend clears ~$4,000**, or when Meta produces leads reliably
enough to fund a second channel from revenue.

## What to do now — free, and already half-working

### Peter's personal profile is the strongest asset here

Personal profiles massively out-reach company pages on LinkedIn, and Peter's posts are
already the best-written content in the account. The Windows 365 post — "took me three
attempts before I stopped fiddling" — is exactly right.

**Cadence: 2–3 posts/week from Peter.** Company page mirrors the best one.

Rotate four post types:

1. **The incident** — a real client problem, what happened, what you found, what you'd
   do differently. Your proven best performer.
2. **The admission** — something that went wrong, or that you got wrong first time.
   Highest trust-per-word content available to this brand.
3. **The unpopular opinion** — "You probably don't need new cameras." "Most cyber
   audits are designed to sell the quote." Drives comments, which drives reach.
4. **The teardown** — a piece of data (like the retail theft figures) plus what it
   actually means for a business owner.

### Rules that matter on LinkedIn

- **No outbound links in the post body.** LinkedIn suppresses them. Put the link in
  the first comment and say "link in comments."
- **First two lines are everything** — the rest is behind "see more."
- **Reply to every comment within an hour.** Comment velocity in the first 60 minutes
  drives distribution more than anything else.
- **Text and native video beat link posts.** The current pattern of link-heavy posts
  is costing reach.

### Company page

- Post 2–3×/week (can mirror Peter's)
- Complete every profile field — specialties, website, size, industry
- Add the Tech Tuesday series to Featured
- Add all staff as employees; their profiles feed the page

### Free lead generation that actually works here

1. Peter connects with 15–20 targeted people/week: Sydney/AU business owners, ops
   managers, retail and warehouse managers, practice managers. Personalised note, no pitch.
2. Comment substantively on posts by AU business groups and adjacent providers.
3. Post the free calculator (msp.alphalogix.com.au) monthly — a genuinely useful free
   tool is the best organic lead magnet you have and it's underused.

## When paid does start

- Objective: Lead Gen Forms (not website — LinkedIn's native forms convert far better)
- Targeting: job title (Owner, Director, Operations Manager, Practice Manager, IT
  Manager) + company size 11–200 + location Australia
- Budget: minimum $50/day/campaign
- Creative: Script 05 (founder trust) performs better here than the hard-CTA cuts —
  LinkedIn rewards credibility over urgency
