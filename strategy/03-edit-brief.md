# Edit Brief — Hand This to the Editor

## Deliverables per script

Each script produces **9 files**:

| Ratio | Lengths | Where |
|---|---|---|
| 9:16 (1080×1920) | 45s, 15s | Reels, Stories, TikTok, Shorts |
| 1:1 (1080×1080) | 45s, 15s | Feed |
| 16:9 (1920×1080) | 45s | YouTube, website |

Plus **3 hook variants** of the 9:16 45s (first 3s swapped only).

**Master is 9:16.** Frame everything with faces and text inside the centre 80% —
Reels UI eats the top and bottom.

## Non-negotiables

1. **Burned-in captions on every version.** Sans-serif, heavy weight, high contrast,
   with a subtle stroke or shadow so it survives any background. Most views are silent.
2. **Cut on the beat.** Every b-roll change lands on a music transient. It's the
   difference between amateur and professional and costs nothing.
3. **No dead air at the top.** Frame 1 is the first word. Trim every breath before it.
4. **Remove filler.** Cut "um," "so," "you know," and any pause over ~400ms mid-sentence.
   Ruthless trimming is what makes a 60s take fit 45s without feeling rushed.
5. **Logo at the end only**, ≤1.5s, with the tagline lockup.
6. **Loudness ~-14 LUFS.** Music under dialogue at roughly -18 to -20dB. Voice must
   win on a phone speaker at half volume.
7. **Music: neutral, forward-moving, no lyrics, no tension-riser stings.** Fear-based
   audio contradicts the brand. Licensed only — Epidemic Sound or Artlist.

## Visual language

- **Colour:** pull from the existing logo and site. Keep one accent colour and use it
  for every caption highlight and lower third. Consistency across the set is what
  makes six ads read as one brand.
- **Text cards:** flat background in the accent colour, white text, no gradients,
  no drop shadows, no animated wipes. Restraint reads as competence.
- **Do not use:** hooded figures, green matrix code, spinning globes, red alert
  klaxon graphics, generic corporate handshake stock. They signal "template" and
  actively destroy trust for a brand whose whole claim is honesty.

## File naming

```
ALX_<pillar>_<script>_<ratio>_<length>_<variant>.mp4
ALX_CYBER_01_9x16_45s_hookA.mp4
ALX_SURV_03_1x1_15s_v1.mp4
```

Matters more than it seems — you'll be uploading dozens and Ads Manager shows the
filename.

## Delivery

H.264 MP4, 1080p, ~10–12 Mbps, AAC audio 128kbps+. Under 250MB per file for Meta.
Ship the 9:16 45s hero cuts first so campaigns can start while the rest finish.
