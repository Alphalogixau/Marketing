# Facebook & Instagram Review — What's Working, What to Change

Based on the last ~20 posts across FB, IG and LinkedIn (Jul–Aug 2026).

---

## What's genuinely working — protect these

**1. The tagline is better than you're using it.**
"See More. Respond Faster. Stay Secure." maps perfectly onto all three revenue lines:

- **See More** → AI surveillance
- **Respond Faster** → managed IT / support
- **Stay Secure** → cyber security

That is a rare thing: one line that organises the whole business. Right now it sits
at the bottom of posts as a sign-off. It should be the **spine of the brand**, and
each pillar should own a content stream. See `02-positioning.md`.

**2. The incident stories are your best asset.**
The phishing-tender post (28 Jul) is the strongest thing on the account. Real client,
real attack chain, and a genuine twist — the hidden Outlook rule silently marking
incoming mail as read for days. That's not marketing content, that's *proof of
competence*. It builds trust the way the brand claims to.

**3. Peter's LinkedIn voice is the differentiator.**
"Took me three attempts before I stopped fiddling and just let it run overnight."
"I learned this one the hard way." Admitting the mistake is what makes the expertise
credible. Nobody else in this category does that. It's the single most valuable
brand asset here — and it's on the lowest-reach channel.

**4. The retail theft post is a template.**
Data-led, contrarian ("everyone says get AI surveillance — that's not the whole
story"), then a practical evaluation framework. That's exactly the right shape for
surveillance content.

## What's holding it back

### Problem 1 — Everything is top-of-funnel. There is no offer.

Nearly every post ends in "watch the episode," "read the breakdown," "link in bio."
Not one asks for a conversation. The funnel is:

> IG post → bio → YouTube → maybe blog → maybe contact page → maybe enquiry

That's five steps with a drop-off at each. Educational content without a periodic
ask trains the audience to consume and never convert.

**Fix:** Adopt a **4:1 rule** — every fifth post makes a direct, specific offer.
Not "contact us." Something concrete and low-commitment:

- "Free 15-minute security review — we'll tell you what's exposed, even if you never hire us."
- "Free site walkthrough — we'll tell you what your existing cameras can already do."

The second one matters: it *reduces* the sale. That's what makes it credible for a
brand built on honesty.

### Problem 2 — Video length is fighting the content.

The videos are 10–17 seconds carrying stories that deserve 45–60. A 10-second clip
can't land the phishing story, so it becomes a trailer, which pushes people off-platform.

Both Meta and Instagram reward watch time *on platform*. You're systematically
sending your best signal away.

**Fix:** Tell the whole story natively in 45–60s. Put the link second, not first.
Keep YouTube for the long build-along episodes where depth is the point.

### Problem 3 — The Instagram captions have become a formula.

Nearly every IG caption is the same skeleton: hook → ✅ checklist → "link in bio" →
sign-off → hashtag block. It was effective the first three times. It now reads as
templated, and returning followers pattern-match and scroll.

**Fix:** Rotate three caption shapes:
- **Story** — no bullets, just the narrative (the phishing post deserved this)
- **Single question** — one line, invite replies. Comments are ranking signal.
- **List** — keep it, but earn it. Max every third post.

### Problem 4 — Hashtags are too big to work.

`#CyberSecurity`, `#MSP`, `#ManagedIT` are enormous global tags. A page this size
will never surface in them.

**Fix:** 3 broad / 5 niche / 2 local. Favour where an actual Australian buyer looks:
`#SydneyBusiness` `#SmallBusinessAustralia` `#RetailAustralia` `#EssentialEight`
`#ACSC` `#LossPrevention` `#NSWBusiness`. Local intent beats global volume every time.

### Problem 5 — FB and IG are getting identical copy.

The 28 Jul posts are near-identical across both. Different audiences, different norms:
Facebook tolerates longer prose and working links in-post; Instagram doesn't have
clickable links and rewards a stronger opening line.

**Fix:** Keep the Buffer workflow, but write FB and IG separately. It's ~5 extra
minutes per post.

### Problem 6 — Posting is inconsistent in channel.

The CrowdStrike / Dicker Data post (6 Aug) went up natively, not through Buffer, with
a caption that reads as an internal thank-you rather than something aimed at a buyer.
Partner credentials are trust assets — CrowdStrike is a serious badge to be able to
show. Frame it for the customer:

> "We're now a CrowdStrike partner. That means our clients get the same endpoint
> protection the ASX 200 runs — at small-business scale."

### Problem 7 — Nothing is retargetable.

No pixel. Every person who watched a video, read the blog, or used the free
calculator is unreachable. Fix this in week 1 before spending a dollar on ads —
the free calculator at msp.alphalogix.com.au is likely your highest-intent audience
in existence and it is currently invisible.
