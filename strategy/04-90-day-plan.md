# 90-Day Paid Plan

**Budget:** $500–1,500/mo → plan built at **$1,000/mo (~$33/day)**.
**Platform split: 100% Meta. LinkedIn organic only.**

## Why no LinkedIn paid

LinkedIn's minimum viable spend in Australian B2B is roughly $50–80/day *per campaign*
before delivery stabilises. At $33/day total we'd get a few hundred impressions a day
and no learning. It would fail, and we'd wrongly conclude LinkedIn doesn't work.

LinkedIn is already doing real work organically — Peter's personal posts especially.
Revisit paid LinkedIn when monthly spend clears ~$4,000 or when Meta is producing
leads reliably enough to justify a second channel.

## Phase 2 — Weeks 3–6: Cyber, cold (~$33/day)

One campaign, `OUTCOME_LEADS`, **Instant Forms** not website — no pixel history yet,
and on-platform forms convert 3–5× better cold.

- **Ad set A — Sydney metro + 50km.** Age 30–60. Broad (no interests). Advantage+
  Audience on. ~$20/day.
- **Ad set B — AU national, business-interest layer.** ~$13/day.

Creative: Script 01 with hooks A, C, E. Script 02 as the Reels cut.

Sydney first because they're Sydney-based and local social proof helps close.
Broad targeting because Meta's delivery beats hand-picked interests at this budget —
interest stacking fragments an already small budget.

**Realistic expectation:** $30–70 per lead. **10–25 leads/month.** Instant form leads
are lower intent than site enquiries — call within 5 minutes or conversion collapses.

## Phase 3 — Weeks 7–10: Add surveillance (~$33/day, split)

- **Cyber:** $18/day — keep the winner running, don't touch it
- **Surveillance:** $15/day — Script 03, hooks A, B, C

Surveillance targets differently: geo-target retail strips, industrial estates and
logistics precincts; layer interests only if the pool stays large enough. This is the
less contested market and likely the cheaper lead — watch whether it outperforms cyber
and be willing to shift budget toward it.

**Do not split evenly out of fairness.** Follow the data.

## Phase 4 — Weeks 11–13: Retarget + scale (~$33–50/day)

- **Retargeting ad set, ~$8/day.** Script 05 (founder trust) to everyone who
  watched 50%+ or hit the site. Cheapest, highest-converting spend you'll run all quarter.
- Scale the winning cold ad set by **+20% every 3–4 days**. Bigger jumps reset learning.
- Kill anything above 2× target CPL after 1,000+ impressions.

## What "working" looks like

| Metric | Week 4 | Week 8 | Week 13 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cost per lead | <$90 | <$60 | <$45 |
| Leads / month | 8–12 | 15–25 | 20–35 |
| 3-sec view rate | >20% | >25% | >30% |
| Retargeting pool | 500+ | 2,000+ | 5,000+ |
| Booked meetings | 2–4 | 5–8 | 8–12 |

These are honest numbers for a cold account at $1k/month in Australian B2B, not
best-case. **One managed services client typically pays for the entire quarter.**

## Rules

1. **Don't touch a campaign for 5 days after launch.** Meta needs ~50 events to exit
   learning. Editing resets it. This is the most common way small budgets are wasted.
2. **Judge creative on 3-second view rate first, CPL second.** A great offer behind a
   bad hook looks like a bad offer.
3. **Change one thing at a time.**
4. **Speed to lead beats everything.** Under 5 minutes. A same-day callback converts
   several times better than next-day, and no amount of ad optimisation makes up for
   a slow follow-up.
5. **Weekly review, monthly decision.** Look weekly; change strategy monthly.

## If budget is $500/mo instead

Cut Phase 3. Run cyber only for all 90 days at ~$16/day, one ad set, Sydney only.
Expect 5–10 leads/month. Adding surveillance at that level would starve both.
