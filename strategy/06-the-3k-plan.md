# The $3,000/Month Plan

**This is the live plan.** Supersedes `04-90-day-plan.md` and replaces the Tier 1
sketch in `05-domination-plan.md`.

$3k does not saturate a segment. So we stop trying to, and win a narrower fight instead.

---

## The one decision that makes $3k work

**Spend nothing on cold prospecting. Spend everything on capturing demand that already exists.**

At $10k you buy awareness *and* capture. At $3k, trying to do both means losing at
both — you get too little frequency to build awareness, and too little budget to own
the searches that convert.

So the allocation follows from one observation: **you already have a demand-creation
engine, and it's free.** Tech Tuesday, the blog, YouTube, LinkedIn, Peter's personal
profile. That machine builds awareness at zero media cost and it's already running.

Which means paid money has exactly one job: **catch people at the moment they're
looking, and close the ones who hesitate.**

```
Organic content  →  builds awareness        (free, already running)
Google Search    →  captures active demand  (paid, $1,800)
Meta retargeting →  closes the hesitators   (paid, $600)
```

No cold Meta. No LinkedIn ads. No display. No Performance Max. Every dollar sits at
the bottom of the funnel where $3k can actually win.

## Allocation

| Line | Monthly | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Google Search** | **$1,800** | Demand capture. Highest intent, fastest payback. |
| **Meta retargeting** | **$600** | Cheapest conversions you will ever buy. |
| **Landing pages + tracking** | **$400** | One-off build, then hosting/tooling. |
| **Contingency / testing** | **$200** | Keyword expansion, new angles. |

Sydney metro only. Not NSW. Not national.

## What we deliberately do NOT do

Each of these is defensible at $10k and actively harmful at $3k:

- ❌ **Cold Meta prospecting** — needs frequency; $3k can't buy it
- ❌ **LinkedIn ads** — floor CPMs eat the budget with no learning
- ❌ **Performance Max** — Google's black box will spend your budget on junk placements
- ❌ **Broad match keywords** — fastest way to waste $1,800
- ❌ **National targeting** — dilutes to nothing
- ❌ **Awareness/reach campaigns** — that's what the organic engine is for

## Google Search — the structure

Three campaigns. Separate budgets so one can't cannibalise another.

### Campaign 1 — Surveillance capture · $700/mo

**Lead here.** Less competitive than MSP terms, cheaper clicks, project-sized deals
that convert faster and open the door to managed services afterwards.

*Exact/phrase match:*
```
cctv installation sydney          security cameras for business sydney
commercial cctv sydney            retail security cameras
warehouse security cameras        multi site cctv
ai security cameras australia     verkada installer sydney
milestone xprotect installer      genetec integrator sydney
business camera system sydney     loss prevention cameras
```

*Negative keywords (critical — residential traffic will destroy this campaign):*
```
-home -residential -diy -cheap -kmart -bunnings -ring -doorbell
-wireless -battery -solar -jobs -salary -course -how to install
```

### Campaign 2 — Displacement · $500/mo

**The highest-value cluster you can buy, and almost nobody bids on it.** These people
have already decided to leave their provider. Low volume, low CPC, extraordinary intent.

```
change it provider              switching it support company
it provider not responding      unhappy with it support
managed services agreement exit it support contract review
second opinion it security      it provider comparison sydney
```

This is where the honesty positioning wins outright. Someone searching *"IT provider
not responding"* is angry and looking for someone straight with them. That's the brand.

### Campaign 3 — Managed IT capture · $600/mo

Most competitive, highest CPCs. Funded last on purpose.

```
managed it services sydney      it support sydney
outsourced it support           essential eight compliance
microsoft 365 migration sydney  it support for retail
cyber security services sydney  acsc essential eight consultant
```

### Google settings that matter

- **Manual CPC or Maximise Clicks** to start — not Target CPA. No conversion history yet.
- **Exact and phrase match only.** No broad match until you have 90 days of data.
- **Ad schedule: business hours + 1 hour either side.** Don't pay for 2am clicks you
  can't answer.
- **Location: Sydney metro, "people in" not "interested in".**
- **Call extensions, sitelinks, location extensions** — free real estate, lifts CTR.

## Meta retargeting — $600/mo

The cheapest conversions available to you. These people already visited.

| Audience | Creative | Budget |
|---|---|---|
| Site visitors 30d, didn't convert | Script 02 / 04 (15s cutdowns) | $250 |
| Calculator users (msp.alphalogix.com.au) | Script 05 (founder trust) | $200 |
| Video viewers 50%+, IG/FB engagers 365d | Script 01 / 03 (45s heroes) | $150 |

Calculator users are your highest-intent audience in existence — someone costing out
IT spend is actively budgeting. **They are currently untracked.** Fixing that is the
single highest-return hour of work in this plan.

## The thing that will make or break this

**You cannot send Google traffic to your homepage.** It is the most common and most
expensive mistake at this budget.

Three purpose-built landing pages, one per campaign:

1. `/security-cameras-business` → free site walkthrough
2. `/change-it-provider` → free 15-minute review *(this one converts hardest)*
3. `/managed-it-sydney` → free 15-minute review

Each page: one headline matching the search, one offer, one form, proof (CrowdStrike,
Microsoft, five surveillance certifications, published incident write-ups), no navigation
menu to leak clicks.

**A great ad to a bad page loses. A decent ad to a great page wins.**

**The first client pays for two years of the entire programme.**

## Non-negotiables

1. **Speed to lead under 5 minutes.** At $3k there is no slack. A lead that waits
   until tomorrow is money set on fire.
2. **12 months.** Months 1–2 are learning. Killing this in week 6 wastes all of it.
3. **Landing pages before launch.** Do not go live pointing at the homepage.
4. **Check search terms weekly** for the first month. Add negative keywords
   aggressively — this is where wasted spend hides.
5. **Keep the organic engine running.** It's doing the awareness work that the paid
   budget can't afford. If Tech Tuesday stops, this plan gets materially worse.

## When to scale

When cost-per-qualified-lead is stable and you've closed 3+ clients from paid, move
to $5k: add the second geography (Newcastle/Wollongong) and turn on Meta cold
prospecting with the winning hooks. Don't scale on hope — scale on a closed deal.
