# Positioning — The Spine

## The core idea

The brand value is **honesty builds trust**. In a category where every competitor
claims "24/7 support, proactive monitoring, trusted partner," honesty is not a soft
value — it is the only available differentiator, because it's the one thing
competitors can't copy without actually doing it.

Honesty in practice means three uncomfortable, effective things:

1. **Tell people what's wrong even when it doesn't sell.** ("Your existing cameras
   may already do this. Let's check before you spend.")
2. **Show the failures, not just the wins.** (Peter's "took me three attempts" —
   already the highest-trust content on the account.)
3. **Name the thing competitors won't.** (Consumer Windows 11 Home on a business
   machine. Antivirus is not a security strategy.)

## The organising line

**See More. Respond Faster. Stay Secure.**

Already in use, and stronger than it's being asked to be. Each clause owns a pillar:

| Pillar | Line | Offer | Buyer |
|---|---|---|---|
| Surveillance / AI | **See More** | Free site walkthrough | Retail, warehousing, multi-site, strata, schools |
| Managed IT | **Respond Faster** | Free 15-min IT health check | 15–150 seat SMBs, office-based |
| Cyber security | **Stay Secure** | Free 15-min security review | Any SMB, esp. professional services, finance, legal |

Every piece of content should be assignable to exactly one pillar. If it isn't, it's
probably not for a customer.

## Proof assets we can actually claim

Use these; don't invent others.

- Trading since 2022, national — every Australian state
- Peter: ~20 years in IT
- Microsoft Partner; **CrowdStrike partner** (via Dicker Data)
- Certified: Milestone XProtect, Genetec Security Centre, Avigilon, Verkada, Nx Witness
- Deploys Axis and Ubiquiti UniFi camera infrastructure
- Builds to **ACSC Essential Eight** and **CIS Level 1** — this is a genuinely strong,
  specific, checkable claim and it's currently buried in a Windows 365 post
- Documented real incident response with published write-ups
- A free, working tool in market (CapEx/OpEx calculator)

## What we must NOT claim

- Longevity we don't have — the company is 4 years old. Lean on Peter's 20 years,
  attributed to him personally, not the business.
- Client names, logos or numbers not already public.
- Specific response-time SLAs unless they're contractually real.
- Breach statistics without a source. The retail theft post did this correctly
  (595,660 victims, $7.79bn, cited) — hold that standard.

## Tone

Plain Australian English. Short sentences. No "leverage," "solutions," "synergy,"
"empower," "cutting-edge." If a line could appear on any competitor's website
unchanged, cut it.

The test for every script line: **would Peter say this out loud to a client across
a table?** If not, rewrite it.
