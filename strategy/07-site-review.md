# Site Review — What alphalogix.com.au Actually Says

Reviewed August 2026 via search index (the site itself is blocked by this
environment's network egress proxy, so this is drawn from indexed page content
rather than a direct crawl — flagged for honesty).

## The positioning was already yours

The About page states the founding belief as:

> "complex technology problems deserve clear thinking, **honest advice**, and partners
> who are still standing next to you when it gets hard"

and describes the company as *"building a reputation on **honesty and trust**."*

**This is not a positioning I invented — it's already written on your own site.**
Everything in `02-positioning.md` is consistent with what Alphalogix already says
about itself. That's the best possible outcome: the ads aren't a new claim to live up
to, they're an amplification of a promise already made.

## Corrections to earlier assumptions

| Earlier | Actual |
|---|---|
| "Since 2022" | Founded **July 2022** |
| "Peter, ~20 years in IT" | **18+ years** per the site (his LinkedIn says "almost 20") — use **18+**, it's the checkable number |
| "Microsoft and CrowdStrike only" | Considerably more — see below |

## The partner list is much deeper than reported

The site names all of these:

**Security:** CrowdStrike · **Huntress** · Bitdefender · Microsoft Defender / Microsoft Security Suite · **1Password**
**Cloud:** Microsoft Azure · AWS · Google Cloud (certified Microsoft Partner)
**Comms:** **Aussie Broadband** · **Breeze Connect**
**Surveillance:** Milestone XProtect · Genetec Security Centre · Avigilon · Verkada · Nx Witness · Axis · Ubiquiti UniFi

### Two of these are more valuable than they look

**Aussie Broadband.** For an Australian SMB audience this may carry *higher* brand
recognition than CrowdStrike. It's a consumer-visible Australian brand with genuine
affinity, and it signals "local, real, established" instantly — no explanation needed.
Worth testing as a logo alongside Microsoft.

**Huntress.** Doesn't register with a general audience, but it's a strong credential
to a technical buyer or anyone getting a second opinion. Keep it for the site and
proposals, not the ad.

## The missed pillar: Unified Communications

The tagline maps cleanly to three of four services:

| Line | Service |
|---|---|
| See More | Surveillance |
| Respond Faster | Managed IT / support |
| Stay Secure | Cyber security |
| *(unmapped)* | **Unified communications — internet, phones, mobile, collaboration** |

Alphalogix resells internet and runs phone systems. That's a real revenue line and a
recurring one, and none of the strategy documents accounted for it.

**It also makes the competitive wedge stronger than described.** The earlier framing
was "MSPs don't do cameras, integrators don't do M365." The fuller version:

> A multi-site operator typically juggles an internet provider, a phone provider, an
> IT company, a security company and a camera installer. **Alphalogix is all five.**
> One vendor. One bill. One number to call when something breaks.

For a retail or hospitality group with eight sites, that's a stronger argument than
any technical claim — it's an operational relief, and operations people buy relief.

## Consequence for the launch ad — fixed

The video's on-screen text reads **IT Management / Surveillance / Network / Cyber
Security**. The first draft of the captions said "managed IT, cyber security and
surveillance" — **omitting Network**, which the video shows.

A caption that lists fewer services than the video is a visible inconsistency in the
one ad meant to introduce the company. Corrected in `../ad-copy/01-meta-launch-captions.md`:
all variants now read **"IT management, networks, cyber security and surveillance."**

Variant B was also rewritten to lead on the five-vendor consolidation, and its
headline changed from *"One Partner. Not Three."* to **"One Partner. Not Five."**

## Follow-ups worth doing

1. **Add Unified Communications as a fourth pillar** in positioning, with its own
   offer. Likely something like a free bill review across internet, phones and mobile —
   a strong, concrete, low-commitment hook that fits the honesty brand ("we'll tell
   you if you're already on a good deal").
2. **Add UC keywords to Google Ads** — "business internet Sydney", "business phone
   systems Sydney", "VoIP provider Sydney". Not yet in `../google-ads/`.
3. **Test Aussie Broadband as a logo** alongside Microsoft for Australian audiences.
4. **Standardise on 18+ years** wherever Peter's experience is quoted.
