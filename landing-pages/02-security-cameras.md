## Section 1 — The reframe

### H2
### Recording is not the same as watching

Almost every system installed in Australian business is a **recording device**. It
captures footage. Somebody reviews it days later, once the stock is already gone,
the vehicle has already left, and you're filling in an insurance form.

That's not security. That's evidence collection.

**AI surveillance inverts it.** The system watches continuously and tells you at the
moment something happens — not next Tuesday when you go looking.

## Section 2 — The possum problem *(keep this, it's the memorable bit)*

### H2
### Four hundred alerts a night. How many did you check?

The reason most businesses switch off their motion alerts is that motion alerts are
useless. Wind. Rain. Headlights. Cats. Possums.

Modern AI surveillance knows the difference between **a person, a vehicle, and an
animal.** It can flag someone loitering near a fire exit at 2am and ignore the possum
on the bins entirely.

Alerts that mean something get acted on. Alerts that cry wolf get muted — and a muted
system protects nothing.

**What it can do:**
- Person, vehicle and animal classification
- Loitering and after-hours presence detection
- Licence plate recognition
- Line-crossing and restricted zone alerts
- People counting and queue monitoring
- Cross-site search — find a person or vehicle across every camera, every location

## Section 3 — The honest section *(do not cut — this is why the page converts)*

### H2
### You might not need new cameras

Here's what a camera company won't tell you: **a lot of AI surveillance is software,
not hardware.**

If your existing cameras are reasonably modern and decently placed, there's a real
chance the upgrade is a licence and a configuration change — not a truckload of new
equipment and a five-figure install.

Sometimes it genuinely is a rebuild. Old analogue gear, bad angles, and cameras
pointed at the wrong things can't be fixed in software.

**The point of the free walkthrough is to find out which of those you are, before
you commit to anything.** We'd rather sell you a licence you actually need than a
system you don't.
