## Section 1 — Differentiation *(the whole job of this page)*

### H2
### Three things most providers won't do

**1. Tell you what you don't need.**
The standard playbook is a scan, a red report, and a quote attached to it. The report
exists to sell the quote. We'll tell you when something isn't worth doing — including
when your current setup is fine.

**2. Publish our mistakes.**
We write up real client incidents in full, including the things we missed on the first
pass. One recent one: we rebuilt a compromised machine, and only afterwards found a
hidden mail rule silently moving every incoming email into Conversation History and
marking it read. We published that. **[Read the write-ups →]**

**3. Do the cameras too.**
Most MSPs don't touch surveillance and refer it out. We're certified across five
surveillance platforms. If you're multi-site, that's one partner instead of three.

## Section 3 — The honest section

### H2
### Almost every breach we're called into isn't sophisticated

No zero-days. No state actors. It's:

- Someone clicked a link in an email
- A password was reused across services
- An old staff account was never switched off
- A consumer laptop running Windows 11 Home on a business network, unpatched

**That's it. That's how it happens — and all of it is preventable.**

Anyone telling you that you need enterprise-grade threat intelligence before you've
closed those four things is selling you something. Get the basics right first. We'll
tell you which basics you're missing in fifteen minutes, for free.
