## Section 4 — Objection handling

### H2
### The bit everyone worries about

**"Switching will break everything."**
It doesn't, when it's planned. We run migrations in parallel — the new environment
stands up before the old one comes down. Most of our onboardings happen without a
single hour of downtime.

**"I'm locked into a contract."**
Most agreements have a notice period rather than a hard lock. We'll read yours with
you and tell you the real position — including if the honest answer is "wait four months."

**"I don't have time for this."**
Fifteen minutes. One call. If it's not useful, you've lost a quarter of an hour.

**"You're just going to sell me something."**
That's the whole point of the page. We might not be right for you, and we'd rather
find that out in 15 minutes than six months in.
